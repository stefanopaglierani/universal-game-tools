# UWR Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- `UWRWorld`: opens single-file and tiled worlds, streams tiles around a node, applies a ground material, adds an optional sea, and exposes roads, junctions, places and the nearest road point.
- Built-in ground shader (grass, rock, sand, gravel next to roads) with optional textures, and a simple sea shader.
- Fly-over demo, headless tests and two small sample worlds.
